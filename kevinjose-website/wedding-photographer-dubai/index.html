Overview

How I can help.

Your wedding day moves fast. I am there to capture the real moments as they happen: the nerves, the laughter, the quiet looks and the big entrances, in a natural, cinematic style. I photograph and film weddings, engagements and celebrations across Dubai and the UAE, from desert ceremonies to hotel ballrooms and beach venues.

Before the day, we talk through your plans, the people who matter most and the moments you do not want missed. On the day, I work quietly in the background so you can enjoy yourselves. Afterwards you receive a carefully edited gallery and, if you choose film coverage, a highlight film you will want to watch again and again.

FAQ

Yes. You can book photography, film, or both, so your photos and film share the same style.

Where can we do a pre-wedding shoot?

Popular options include the desert, Dubai's old town, the beach and city rooftops. We choose the location together based on the look you want.
