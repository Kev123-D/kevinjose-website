Overview

Working in Sharjah.

Sharjah is only a short drive from Dubai, and I work there often for family businesses, restaurants, retailers, property developers and couples. The emirate has a distinct identity built on culture, heritage and family life, which shapes how brands there should look and speak.

From restaurants along Al Majaz Waterfront and Al Qasba to new homes in Aljada and Muwaileh, and heritage-inspired shoots in the Heart of Sharjah, I bring the same crew, gear and process I use across the UAE.

FAQ

Good questions.

Do you cover Sharjah as well as Dubai?

Yes. Sharjah is part of my regular coverage area, and shoots there are planned and quoted the same way as in Dubai.

Are there rules for filming in public places in Sharjah?

Some locations need permission for commercial filming, and respectful dress and behaviour are expected in public. I check requirements for each location before the shoot.

Can you create Arabic ad campaigns for Sharjah customers?

Yes. Campaigns can run in Arabic and English, targeted to Sharjah or to specific areas within it.
