What affects the price

How quotes are worked out.

Time on set

Half day, full day or several days. Most shoots are priced by the time needed.

Crew and gear

Extra crew, lighting, drones or specialist lenses for bigger productions.

Number of deliverables

How many edited photos, videos, ad versions or design pieces you need.

Location and travel

Shoots across the UAE are covered; projects abroad include travel.

Usage

Where the work will appear, such as social, website, print, outdoor or TV.

Timeline

Rush turnarounds and launch deadlines can affect the plan.

FAQ

Pricing questions.

Do you have fixed packages?

Every project is a little different, so I send a clear quote after a short call. You know exactly what is included before anything is booked.

How quickly can I get a quote?

Usually within a couple of days of hearing about your project. The more detail you share about dates, deliverables and usage, the faster and more accurate the quote.

Do you work with small businesses?

Yes. Many projects can be done in stages, such as a logo and launch photos first, then packaging, a website and ads as the business grows.

Is VAT included?

Quotes state clearly whether VAT is included.
