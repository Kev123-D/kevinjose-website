Overview

How I can help.

Most ad campaigns fail on the creative, not the settings. Agencies often run ads with stock footage or whatever the client sends over. I shoot the ad creative myself, cut it for every placement, then set up, run and optimise the campaigns, so the brand and the ads never drift apart.

Campaigns start with your goal: enquiries, bookings, store visits or online sales. I set up tracking so we can see what each ad actually brings in, target audiences across Dubai and the UAE, and test different hooks, formats and offers. Every month you get a clear report on what was spent, what it brought in and what we will change next.

FAQ

Good questions.

Is ad spend included?

No. Ad spend is paid directly to the platforms (Meta, TikTok, Google, Snapchat) from your own account, separately from the management and creative fee.

Which platform should I advertise on?

It depends on your customers. Meta suits most consumer brands, TikTok and Snapchat reach younger audiences, and Google Ads catches people already searching for what you sell. I recommend a mix after a short discovery call.

How soon will I see results?

Campaigns usually need a testing period to learn which audiences and creatives work best before they are scaled. You will see results reported from the first month.

Do you create the ad content?

Yes. That is the main difference: the photos and videos for your ads are shot and edited in-house.
