Case study · Butchery & food · Beirut, Lebanon

Sama Beirut.

Butchery & more. A food brand designed from scratch.

ClientSama Beirut
SectorButchery & food
LocationBeirut, Lebanon
ScopeFull brand build, photography & website

The brief

Where it started.

Sama Beirut is a butchery and food brand in Beirut that needed an identity strong enough to work on a shopfront, on the side of a moving delivery van and on a meat box at the customer's door.

The brand had to signal freshness, quality and local pride, feel bold and appetite-driven in photography, and make ordering online simple for regular customers.

What I did

Every piece, in-house.

Logo and identity

A cleaver-and-cedar logo that joins the craft of the butcher with a symbol of Lebanon, supported by a bold colour palette and typography.

Guidelines

An identity system with rules for the logo, colours, type and photography so every application stays consistent.

Signage and vehicle livery

The shopfront signage and the delivery van, designed to be recognised from across the street.

Packaging and print

Meat boxes, food trays, paper bags, bakery bags and business cards in one matching system.

Food photography

A bold, appetite-driven style for the products, plus interior mood images of the shop.

Website and online ordering

A website where customers can browse products and order online for delivery.

The work

From identity to launch.

Deliverables & outcome

What came out of it.

The result is a single identity that runs from the shopfront and the delivery van to the box on the customer's table and the online ordering page. The food photography and packaging also became the creative for Meta carousel ads, built around fresh cuts, the BBQ box and delivery.
