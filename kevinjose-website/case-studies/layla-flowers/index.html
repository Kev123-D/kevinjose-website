Case study · Florist · Dubai, UAE

Layla Flowers.

A Dubai florist brand, from a blank page to launch.

ClientLayla Flowers
SectorFlorist
LocationDubai, UAE
ScopeFull brand build, photography & website

The brief

Where it started.

Layla Flowers needed a complete brand for a florist in Dubai, starting from nothing: no name, no logo and no visual style. Dubai is a city where flowers are bought as gifts for every occasion, and where the box, the wrapping and the card are often photographed and shared as much as the bouquet itself.

The brand had to feel soft, romantic and premium, work in a busy Instagram feed and on a small phone screen, and carry through every piece a customer touches, from the website to the ribbon on the bouquet.

What I did

Every piece, in-house.

Name and positioning

A short, warm, easy-to-say name paired with a clear descriptor, so customers know what the business sells the moment they hear it.

Logo and identity

A logo, colour palette and typography built around a soft, romantic mood, with a pattern that can carry the brand even when the logo is not visible.

Brand guidelines

Rules for the logo, colours, typefaces, photography and tone, so every printer and future designer produces work that matches.

Packaging and print

Patterned wrapping paper, embroidered and printed ribbon, gift tags in two colourways and message cards, all designed to be photographed.

Product and lifestyle photography

Bouquets, rose boxes, orchids and tulips shot in one consistent style for the website, social media and ads.

Website and ecommerce

An online store to browse and order arrangements, built around the photography and the brand palette.

The work

From identity to launch.

Deliverables & outcome

What came out of it.

The result is one complete brand, from the first sketch of the logo to the online store, where the packaging, the photography and the website all clearly belong together. The same photography was then cut into Instagram carousel ads and Google Search ads, so the brand and the advertising never drift apart.
