Meta Ads vs Google Ads for UAE Businesses

A plain-language comparison to help you decide where to spend your first ad budget, and how the two platforms work together.

By Kevin Jose8 min readDubai, UAE

The short answer

Google ads reach people who are already searching for what you sell. Meta ads, on Instagram and Facebook, reach people who are not searching yet but match the kind of customer you want. Google captures demand that already exists; Meta creates new demand. Many UAE businesses end up using both, but which one you start with depends on your product and your goals.

How Google ads work

With Google Search ads you choose the keywords your customers type, such as "flower delivery Dubai" or "villa photographer Abu Dhabi", and your ad can appear at the top of the results. You usually pay when someone clicks. Google also offers YouTube video ads, display ads on websites, and Performance Max campaigns that run across all of Google's channels.

  • Strengths: high intent, because people are actively looking; good for urgent needs and services.
  • Limits: only works if people already search for your product; popular keywords in Dubai can be expensive.

How Meta ads work

Meta ads appear in Instagram and Facebook feeds, Stories and Reels. Instead of keywords, you target people by location, age, interests and behaviour, or let Meta's system find people similar to your existing customers. The creative, meaning the photo or video itself, does most of the work, because you have to stop someone who was scrolling for something else.

  • Strengths: very visual, great for products, food, fashion, beauty, hospitality and real estate; can build awareness of a brand nobody has heard of yet.
  • Limits: people are not actively looking, so you need strong creative and a clear offer; results depend heavily on the quality of the visuals.

Which works best for which business

  • Urgent or need-based services, such as repairs, movers, clinics or legal services: start with Google Search, because customers search when they need you.
  • Visual products and lifestyle brands, such as florists, restaurants, fashion and beauty: start with Meta, and add TikTok where your audience is younger.
  • Real estate and holiday homes: both work. Meta is strong for showing properties and building lead lists; Google captures people searching for specific areas and property types.
  • A brand-new product nobody searches for yet: start with Meta or TikTok to create awareness, then add Google once people begin searching for your name.
  • Ecommerce: Meta for discovery and Google Shopping and Search for people ready to buy, usually together.

Targeting in the UAE

Both platforms let you target by emirate, city or a radius around your location, which is useful in a country where people in Dubai Marina and Mirdif can behave very differently. The UAE is also highly multilingual, so consider running ads in English and Arabic, and test other languages where they match your customers. Think about timing too: Ramadan, Eid, the summer months, Dubai Shopping Festival and the National Day period can all change how people shop.

Creative matters more than you think

On Meta and TikTok, the photo or video is usually the biggest factor in whether an ad works. A professional product shot, a short video cut for vertical Stories and Reels, and a carousel of several products give the platform more to work with. On Google Search the "creative" is mostly text, so clear headlines, a strong offer and a fast, relevant landing page matter most. Read our guide to brand photography for how to plan visuals that work in ads.

Measure what matters

Before spending, decide what a result is: a sale, a booking, a WhatsApp message, a call or a form. Install the Meta Pixel and Google tag on your website, set up conversion tracking, and judge each platform on cost per result rather than likes or clicks. Start with a test budget, let campaigns run long enough to learn, then move money towards what works.

Using both together

The two platforms support each other. Someone might see your Instagram ad, remember the name, and search for you on Google a few days later. Retargeting lets you show ads on Meta to people who visited your website from Google, and the reverse. A simple setup for many small UAE businesses is Google Search for people already looking, and Meta for building awareness and retargeting visitors.

Frequently asked questions

Which is cheaper, Meta or Google ads?

There is no fixed answer. Costs depend on your industry, competition, targeting and creative. Judge each platform by the cost of a real result such as a sale or a lead, not the cost of a click.

How much should I spend on ads in Dubai?

Start with a test budget you are comfortable with for at least a few weeks, measure the cost per result, and scale up what works. I can suggest a starting budget once I know your goals.

Should I run ads in Arabic as well as English?

If your customers include Arabic speakers, yes. Testing both languages often shows which one performs better for your audience.
