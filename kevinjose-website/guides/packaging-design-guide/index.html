Packaging guide

Packaging Design for Small Businesses

Packaging is where customers physically hold your brand. Done well, it protects the product, sells it and gets shared on social media.

By Kevin Jose6 min readDubai, UAE

Why packaging matters

For food, gifting and retail brands, packaging is often the most-seen part of the brand. It sits on desks, gets carried through malls, and is photographed for Instagram and unboxing videos. A memorable bag or box is advertising you have already paid for.

Start with the practical

Before the design, decide what the packaging must do: protect, keep food warm or cold, fit a delivery bag, stack on a shelf or be reused. Talk to suppliers early about sizes, materials, minimum order quantities and lead times, because these shape the design.

Design a system, not one item

Most brands need a family of pieces: bags, boxes, labels, stickers, ribbons, tags and cards. Design them together so they share colours, logo placement and patterns. Layla Flowers uses patterned wrapping paper, a printed ribbon, navy and blush tags and a message card that all work together, so every bouquet is recognisable.

Simple ways to make packaging memorable

  • A signature pattern or colour that covers the whole surface
  • A printed ribbon, sticker or seal instead of fully printed boxes, to keep costs low
  • A short message inside the lid or on a card
  • Bilingual Arabic and English text where it suits your customers

Photograph it properly

Once the packaging arrives, photograph it for your website, social media and ads. Good brand photography of your packaging is often what makes people want to order.

Packaging for food and delivery

Food packaging must work in Dubai's heat and on delivery bikes. Choose materials that keep food at the right temperature, seal well and do not go soggy, then brand them with sleeves, stickers or printed bags. Clear labelling of contents, allergens and dates builds trust, and should follow the labelling requirements that apply to your products in the UAE.

Gifting packaging

Gifting is a large part of Dubai's retail culture, especially around Ramadan, Eid, Diwali, Christmas and corporate events. Seasonal sleeves, ribbons or cards let you refresh packaging for each occasion without reprinting everything, and a handwritten-style message card makes a gift feel personal.

Working with packaging suppliers

Ask suppliers for physical samples before ordering, confirm minimum order quantities and lead times, and check how colours print on the chosen material, since kraft paper, white board and fabric all change how a colour looks. Order a small first batch where possible, test it with real products and real deliveries, then scale up.

Checklist before you print

  • Logo, colours and fonts match your brand guidelines
  • Sizes tested with real products
  • Text proofread in every language
  • Colours checked on a printed sample
  • Artwork supplied on the printer's template with bleed

Frequently asked questions

How can a small business afford branded packaging?

Start with low-cost pieces such as printed stickers, stamps, ribbons and tags on plain bags and boxes, then move to fully printed packaging as volumes grow.

Is sustainable packaging worth it?

Many customers notice and appreciate recyclable or reusable packaging, and it can become part of your brand story.

What files does a packaging printer need?

Vector artwork on the printer's template, with CMYK or Pantone colours and bleed, which your designer should prepare.
