Overview

Working in Abu Dhabi.

Based in Dubai, I regularly work in Abu Dhabi for brands, hotels, restaurants, property owners and couples. The capital has its own character: a calmer pace than Dubai, a strong hospitality and culture scene, and a growing number of new communities and waterfront developments.

Whether you are launching a restaurant on Al Maryah Island, listing a villa on Saadiyat or Yas Island, filming a brand story near the Corniche or planning a wedding at a resort, I bring the same crew, gear and process as for any shoot in Dubai.

FAQ

Yes. Many hotels require advance permission for commercial shoots, so I plan the timing and access with you or the venue before the day.

Yes. Meta, TikTok and Google campaigns can target Abu Dhabi specifically, or particular areas within it, in English and Arabic.
