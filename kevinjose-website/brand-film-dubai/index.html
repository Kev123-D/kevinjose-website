Overview

How I can help.

A brand film is often the first time a customer meets you properly. It shows who you are, how you work and why you are different, in a way a photo or a paragraph cannot. I plan, shoot and edit brand films for businesses across Dubai and the UAE, from new launches and restaurants to hotels, retail brands and property developers.

Every film is built around one clear idea. We agree the story first, then plan the shots, locations and people so the shoot day runs smoothly. After the main film is finished, I cut it down into short versions for Instagram Reels, TikTok, YouTube and paid ads, so one shoot gives you content for months.

FAQ

Good questions.

How long does a brand film take to make?

It depends on the scope. Most single-location films are shot in one day, and we agree the edit timeline before the shoot so you know exactly when the final film and cutdowns arrive.

Do you film outside Dubai?

Yes. I film across the UAE, including Abu Dhabi, Sharjah and the Northern Emirates, and travel for projects abroad.

Can the film be used for ads?

Yes. Every brand film can come with short ad edits designed for Meta, TikTok and YouTube, and I can run the campaigns for you as well.

Who writes the script?

I write the concept and script with you, based on a short brief about your brand, your customers and what the film needs to achieve.
