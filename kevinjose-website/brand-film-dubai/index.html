Overview

How I can help.

A brand film is often the first time a customer meets you properly. It shows who you are, how you work and why you are different, in a way a photo or a paragraph cannot. I plan, shoot and edit brand films for businesses across Dubai and the UAE, from new launches and restaurants to hotels, retail brands and property developers.

Every film is built around one clear idea. We agree the story first, then plan the shots, locations and people so the shoot day runs smoothly. After the main film is finished, I cut it down into short versions for Instagram Reels, TikTok, YouTube and paid ads, so one shoot gives you content for months.

The kinds of film I make most often in Dubai are brand story films that introduce a business and the people behind it, product and launch films for new collections and menus, restaurant and hospitality films that show the space, the food and the service, property and hotel films for developers and holiday-home companies, and short campaign films made specifically to run as paid social ads. Each one is shot on Sony FX3 cinema cameras with professional lighting and sound, so it looks right on a big screen as well as a phone.

Because I also build brands and run digital ads, the film is planned around how it will actually be used. The colours, tone and pacing follow your brand guidelines, the opening seconds are designed to stop the scroll on Instagram and TikTok, and the edits are cut to the exact lengths each ad platform rewards. For brands like Sama Beirut and Layla Flowers, the same shoot produced the website hero, the launch reels and the ad creative, so everything matched.

A typical project runs in four stages: a short discovery call and written brief, a concept with script, shot list and location plan, one or more shoot days anywhere in Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Sharjah or the wider UAE, and the edit, with a round of feedback on each version. You receive the master film in 4K, social cutdowns in 9:16, 1:1 and 16:9, subtitle files, and stills pulled from the footage that you can use on your website and socials.

FAQ

Good questions.

How long does a brand film take to make?

It depends on the scope. Most single-location films are shot in one day, and we agree the edit timeline before the shoot so you know exactly when the final film and cutdowns arrive.

Do you film outside Dubai?

Yes. I film across the UAE, including Abu Dhabi, Sharjah and the Northern Emirates, and travel for projects abroad.

What do I receive at the end?

A 4K master film, social cutdowns in vertical, square and widescreen formats, subtitle files, and a selection of stills taken from the footage.

Can the film be used for ads?

Yes. Every brand film can come with short ad edits designed for Meta, TikTok and YouTube, and I can run the campaigns for you as well.

Who writes the script?

I write the concept and script with you, based on a short brief about your brand, your customers and what the film needs to achieve.
