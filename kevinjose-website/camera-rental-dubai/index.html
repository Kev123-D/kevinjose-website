Overview

How I can help.

Buying professional cameras and lighting for a single project rarely makes sense. Renting lets you use the right kit for the job without the cost of owning it. I rent out cameras, lenses, lighting and grip in Dubai to photographers, filmmakers, content creators and production teams.

All equipment is the kit used on real client shoots, so it is maintained, tested and ready to work. Tell me what you are shooting and I can suggest the right setup. Rentals are available by the day or over the weekend, with pickup and drop-off in Dubai.
