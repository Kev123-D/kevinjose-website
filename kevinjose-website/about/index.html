About

Hi, I'm Kevin.

A Dubai-based photographer and filmmaker who also builds brands and runs the ads that launch them. One person from the first sketch of a logo to the campaign that puts it in front of customers.

The story

I have been shooting professionally since 2018, working with brands, restaurants, property owners and couples across Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Sharjah and the Northern Emirates, and on projects abroad, including brand work in Beirut.

Over time, clients kept asking for more than photos. They needed a logo that matched the photos, packaging that looked good on camera, a website to put it all on, and ads to get it seen. So I built that into the way I work. Today I cover the whole chain myself: strategy and identity, photography and film, websites and the paid campaigns on Meta, TikTok and Google.

The benefit for clients is simple. The person who designs your brand is the same person who shoots it and advertises it, so nothing gets lost between agencies and everything looks like it belongs together.

How I work

Every project starts with a short call about your business, your customers and your goals. You get a clear plan and quote before anything starts, the work is done properly and on schedule, and you receive final files in every format you need.

I shoot on Sony cinema and mirrorless cameras, and the same gear is available to rent for other creators in Dubai.
